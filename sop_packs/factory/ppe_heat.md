# SYNTHETIC SOP FOR DEMO - not an official policy

## PPE Requirements
Workers in the production area must wear the required gloves, eyewear, and helmet before entering the zone.

## Heat Stress Thresholds
If the ambient temperature exceeds the safe operating threshold or the worker shows signs of heat stress, rotate the task and move the person to a cooler area.

## Shift Rotation
Schedule frequent rotation for jobs involving sustained high heat exposure and ensure hydration breaks are taken.

## Medical Response
If a worker reports dizziness or weakness, send them to the medical room and notify the supervisor immediately.
