# SYNTHETIC SOP FOR DEMO - not an official policy

## Detection & Verification
Log the complaint confidentially through a designated reporting channel and assess immediate safety needs before recommending further action.

## Reporting Channels
Students or staff may file a complaint through the warden, the hostel office, or the anti-ragging committee. Reports must be logged confidentially.

## Immediate Response
If a complaint is received, separate the persons involved and ensure the reporting student is not exposed to further contact.

## Committee Escalation
Escalate the matter to the anti-ragging committee and the disciplinary office if the complaint involves repeated harassment or intimidation.

## Confidentiality
Handle all allegations with strict confidentiality and protect the identity of the reporting student unless required for safety reasons.

## Follow-up
Record the final outcome, the support provided to the complainant, and any required monitoring steps.
