# SYNTHETIC SOP FOR DEMO - not an official policy

## Detection & Verification
The officer must confirm the source of the alarm before taking action. If smoke is reported in a room, verify the report with a second source when possible.

## Alarm & Evacuation
Raise the nearest alarm and initiate evacuation if smoke or fire is confirmed. Keep exit paths clear and guide occupants to the designated assembly point.

## Isolation of Power
If an electrical hazard is suspected, isolate power only after the area is checked and the officer has confirmed it is safe to do so.

## Fire Warden Duties
The fire warden must log the report, coordinate the evacuation, and inform the supervisor of any fire safety issue.

## Post-Incident Report
Document all observations and notify the administrator after the incident is resolved.
